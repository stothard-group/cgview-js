Label methods

Adaptive

Current v1.9 baseline. Clean labels follow an oval on a complete circular map or stay near the visible arc on zoomed maps. Increasing density adds labels and moves crowded regions toward straight leaders. Labels animate as the layout changes.

Method details and provenance

Classic

Current v1.9 baseline. Straight leaders place labels in outward levels. Clean uses one level; increasing density allows more levels. Shares density, spacing, and viewport controls with Adaptive.

Method details and provenance

Font size and line thickness apply to both panels. Density, spacing, and Keep in view are independent for each panel and stay the same when switching methods.

Labels drawn includes inline and outside labels, including clipped outside labels when Keep in view is off. Text overlaps, leader crossings, and leaders through text are checked for outside labels after movement settles. Label draw time measures the synchronous draw call; Adaptive layout work across animation frames is shown in the diagnostics.
